---
title: "Delete Workspace"
api: "DELETE /v1/api/workspaces/{workspaceId}"
description: "Delete a workspace you own, along with its call flows, agents, knowledge bases and phone numbers. Master workspaces and your only remaining workspace can't be deleted."
---

<Warning>
Deleting a workspace also removes everything in it:

- **Phone numbers.** Numbers from your plan's free allowance and numbers you brought yourself are released immediately and are **not** brought back by a restore. Paid numbers have their renewal cancelled and stay until the end of the period you've already paid for.
- **Call flows, agents and knowledge bases** are deleted (soft-deleted, so a restore brings them back).
- **The workspace wallet** is deleted with the workspace (also restorable).
- **External members** lose their roles in the workspace, and their invitations are removed.

Anyone whose current workspace was this one is moved to the parent (master) workspace.
</Warning>

### Path Parameters

<ParamField path="workspaceId" type="string" required>
  The `_id` of the workspace to delete.
</ParamField>

### Headers

<ParamField header="x-workspace-id" type="string" required>
  A workspace where your role has the `workspace:delete` permission. The permission is checked against this header, not the path, so this is normally your master workspace.
</ParamField>

### Rules

- You must be the **owner** of the workspace. Team members get `403`.
- The workspace can't be your **master workspace**.
- It can't be your **only** workspace.

### Response Fields

<ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
  `true` when the workspace was deleted.
</ResponseField>

### Errors

| Status | Body | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `403` | `{"error": "Cannot delete your only workspace. You must have at least one workspace."}` | It's your only workspace (checked first) |
| `403` | `{"error": "Cannot delete the master workspace"}` | It's your master workspace |
| `403` | `{"error": "Only the workspace owner can delete the workspace"}` | You aren't the owner |
| `403` | `{"error": "Insufficient permissions", ...}` | Your role in the `x-workspace-id` workspace lacks `workspace:delete` |
| `404` | `{"error": "Workspace not found"}` | No workspace with that ID that you can see |
| `500` | `{"error": "..."}` | Unexpected server error |

### Restoring a deleted workspace

The owner can restore a deleted workspace within **30 days** with `POST /v1/api/workspaces/{workspaceId}/restore` (no body, same `x-workspace-id` rule as delete). Its call flows, agents, knowledge bases and wallet come back, and paid numbers whose renewal was cancelled are renewed again where possible. The response is `{"success": true, "workspace": {...}, "numbers": {"reinstated": 1, "failed": 0}}`.

Restore errors carry a `code`: `403 NOT_OWNER`, `400 MASTER_WORKSPACE`, `409 ALREADY_ACTIVE`, `410 RECOVERY_WINDOW_EXPIRED` (with `recoverableUntil`), `423 WORKSPACE_CHANGE_IN_PROGRESS` (retry shortly), and `404` if no deleted workspace with that ID exists. If restoring would take you over your plan's workspace limit, it returns `402` with `requiresPayment: true`.

<ResponseExample>
```json
{
  "success": true
}
```
</ResponseExample>
