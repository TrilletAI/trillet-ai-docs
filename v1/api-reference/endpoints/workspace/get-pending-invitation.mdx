---
title: "Get Pending Invitations"
api: "GET /v1/api/workspaces/invitations/pending"
description: "List the pending workspace invitations sent to your email address."
---

Returns every pending, unexpired invitation whose recipient email matches yours, across all workspaces.

### Headers
<ParamField header="x-workspace-id" type="string" required>
  The `_id` of any workspace you're a member of. Required with API-key authentication (`400` without it). It doesn't filter the results.
</ParamField>

### Response Fields

<ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
  `true`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="invitations" type="array">
  Your pending invitations.

  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">
      The invitation ID.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="email" type="string">
      The invited email (yours).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="role" type="string">
      The role name you'll get, for example `editor`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string">
      `internal` or `external`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="workspace" type="object">
      `{id, name, initials, isWhiteLabel, companyName}` of the inviting workspace.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="invitedBy" type="object">
      `{id, name, email, picture}` of the person who invited you.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">
      When the invitation was sent.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="expiresAt" type="string">
      When the invitation expires (7 days after it was sent).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="token" type="string">
      The invitation token used to accept it.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
