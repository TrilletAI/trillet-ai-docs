---
title: "Get WhatsApp Connection Status"
api: "GET /v1/api/meta-whatsapp/status"
description: "Check whether a WhatsApp Business Account is connected to your workspace."
---

<Note>
Your role needs the `workspace:view` permission. Otherwise the request fails with `403 {"error": "Insufficient permissions", "requiredPermission": "workspace:view"}`.
</Note>

## Headers
<ParamField header="x-workspace-id" type="string" required>
Workspace identifier for the API.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="metaWhatsApp" type="object">
The workspace's WhatsApp connection. Every field except `isConnected` is `null` when nothing is connected.

<Expandable title="properties">

<ResponseField name="isConnected" type="boolean">
Whether a WhatsApp Business Account is connected.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="wabaId" type="string">
The WhatsApp Business Account ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="wabaName" type="string">
The WhatsApp Business Account name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="businessId" type="string">
The Meta Business ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="businessName" type="string">
The Meta Business name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connectedAt" type="string">
When the account was connected (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connectedBy" type="string">
The `_id` of the user who connected it.
</ResponseField>

</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Body | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `403` | `{"error": "Insufficient permissions"}` | Your role lacks `workspace:view` |
| `404` | `{"message": "Workspace not found"}` | The workspace doesn't exist |
| `500` | `{"message": "Error fetching Meta WhatsApp workspace status"}` | Unexpected server error |
