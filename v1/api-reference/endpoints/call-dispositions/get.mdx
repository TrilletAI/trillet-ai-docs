---
title: "Get Call Disposition"
api: "GET /v1/api/call-dispositions/{id}"
description: "Retrieve one call disposition record by its ID."
---

A call disposition record tracks one contact in a [batch call](/v1/api-reference/endpoints/calls/batch/overview) with smart callbacks turned on: the outcome of each attempt, and when the next callback is due. A record is created after the contact's first call.

## Headers
<ParamField header="x-workspace-id" type="string" required>
Workspace identifier for the API.
</ParamField>

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
The record's `_id`, from [List Call Dispositions](/v1/api-reference/endpoints/call-dispositions/list).
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="_id" type="string">
The record ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="phoneNumber" type="string">
The contact's phone number.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="agentId" type="string">
The agent making the calls.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="batchId" type="string">
The batch the contact belongs to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="workspaceId" type="string">
The workspace the record belongs to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
- `active`: callbacks are still being placed.
- `inactive`: no more callbacks. The contact reached a final outcome, used up `maxAttempts`, or a retry couldn't be placed.
- `cancelled`: the queued callback was cancelled, by [Delete Call Disposition](/v1/api-reference/endpoints/call-dispositions/delete) or by stopping the batch.
- `completed`: Delete Call Disposition was called after the last queued callback had already run.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="currentAttempt" type="number">
How many attempts have been made. `-1` means no further attempts will be made.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="maxAttempts" type="number">
The most attempts the contact will get, from the batch's `smartCallbackConfig`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="dispositionHistory" type="array">
One entry per call, oldest first.

<Expandable title="properties">
<ResponseField name="outcome" type="string">
The outcome of that call, for example `"No Answer"`, `"Voicemail"`, `"Partial Complete: Call Back"` or `"Completed Call"`. The full list is under the `disposition` filter in [List Call Dispositions](/v1/api-reference/endpoints/call-dispositions/list).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="notes" type="string">
A short summary of the call.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="callHistoryId" type="string">
The call history record for that call. `null` on attempts recorded before this field was added.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">
When the outcome was recorded (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="timezone" type="string">
The timezone callback times are worked out in: the agent's office-hours timezone, or `UTC`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="nextCallTime" type="string">
The delay before the next callback, as `"<milliseconds>,<readable>"` (for example `"7200000,0d 2h 0m 0s"`), measured from `currentTimeOfGivenTimezone`. `null` or empty when no callback is due.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="currentTimeOfGivenTimezone" type="string">
The local time, in `timezone`, when the next callback was scheduled (`yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss`). `null` when no callback is due.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="jobId" type="string">
The queued callback job, or `null` when none is queued.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="logs" type="array">
Internal log of queued callback jobs (`jobId`, `outcome`, `createdAt`).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="OfficeHoursSettings" type="object">
The office hours that callbacks are kept within, copied from the agent when the record was created.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">
When the record was created (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string">
When the record last changed (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Body | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `{"error": "Workspace ID is required"}` | The `x-workspace-id` header is missing |
| `404` | `{"error": "Call disposition not found"}` | No record with that ID exists in this workspace |
