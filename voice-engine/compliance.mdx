## HIPAA

Trillet supports HIPAA workloads. A Business Associate Agreement is available on Agency and Enterprise accounts, including Trillet OEM partners, at no extra fee. Execute the BAA before any tenant processes protected health information. See our [Terms of Use](https://trillet.ai/terms-of-use).

A call flow in HIPAA mode keeps call recording off. [Call flow settings](/voice-engine/call-flow-settings) refuses `recording.enabled: true` on such a flow with `409` `hipaa_recording_conflict`.

## Data residency

Each tenant is created in a region, `us` or `au`. Its call records, transcripts and recordings are stored in that region. Events are stored in the tenant's region too, and hold references rather than transcripts: webhook payloads are built from the tenant's regional data when they are delivered.

## Data in events

Call events include transcripts, summaries, extracted data and variables by default. For each webhook endpoint you can:

- turn conversation data off (`includeConversationData: false`), or
- keep it and mask emails, phone numbers and card-like numbers (`redactPII: true`). Masking is pattern based and best effort.

Recordings are never included in payloads. A payload carries a link to the recording download endpoint, which needs a partner key with `resources:read`. See [Events](/voice-engine/events#conversation-data).

The event log keeps events for 30 days.

## Isolation

- Each tenant is a separate workspace. A partner key can reach only its own tenants, and a key can be restricted further to a list of tenants.
- Requests naming another partner's tenant return `404`.
- Partner keys are stored as hashes. Webhook signing secrets are stored encrypted.
- Tenant lifecycle changes (create, update, suspend, resume, delete, restore) and phone number purchases, registrations and releases made through Trillet OEM are written to the tenant's audit log with the ID of the key that made them. Writing the audit entry is best effort: if it fails, the change still goes ahead. Call flow settings changes, webhook endpoint changes, and changes made through [standard endpoints](/voice-engine/authentication#calling-standard-endpoints-for-a-tenant) are not written to the tenant's audit log.

## Trillet staff access

- Trillet's support account is an administrator of your partner account's own workspace.
- It is not a member of your tenants by default. When Trillet support needs to work in a tenant, Trillet staff give the support account administrator access to that one tenant, and can remove it again. Each grant and removal is written to that tenant's audit log, with the staff member who made it.

## Branding

When your account has white-label enabled, new tenants inherit your branding: company name, logo, colours and theme. Webhook requests are sent from Trillet to your servers and identify Trillet (for example in the `User-Agent` header); your customers do not see them.
