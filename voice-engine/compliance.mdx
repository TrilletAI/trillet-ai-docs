## HIPAA

Trillet supports HIPAA workloads. A Business Associate Agreement is available on Agency and Enterprise accounts, including Voice Engine partners, at no extra fee. Execute the BAA before any tenant processes protected health information. See our [Terms of Use](https://trillet.ai/terms-of-use).

## Data residency

Each tenant is created in a region, `us` or `au`. Its call records, transcripts and recordings are stored in that region. Events are stored in the tenant's region too, and hold references rather than transcripts: webhook payloads are built from the tenant's regional data when they are delivered.

## Data in events

Call events include transcripts, summaries, extracted data and variables by default. For each webhook endpoint you can:

- turn conversation data off (`includeConversationData: false`), or
- keep it and mask emails, phone numbers and card-like numbers (`redactPII: true`). Masking is pattern based and best effort.

Recordings are never included in payloads. A payload carries a link to the recording download endpoint, which needs a partner key with `resources:read`. See [Events](/voice-engine/events#conversation-data).

The event log keeps events for 30 days.

## Isolation

- Each tenant is a separate workspace. A partner key can reach only its own tenants, and a key can be restricted further to a list of tenants.
- Requests naming another partner's tenant return `404`.
- Partner keys are stored as hashes. Webhook signing secrets are stored encrypted.
- Tenant and phone number changes made through the Voice Engine API are written to the tenant's audit log with the ID of the key that made them.

## Branding

When your account has white-label enabled, new tenants inherit your branding: company name, logo, colours and theme. Webhook requests are sent from Trillet to your servers and identify Trillet (for example in the `User-Agent` header); your customers do not see them.
