Changes to the Trillet OEM API and its documentation, newest first. Within `/partner/v1` changes are additive, so existing integrations keep working. The `Trillet-Version` response header carries the date of the release that served each request.

<Update label="Provisioning" description="Trillet-Version: 2026-10-28" tags={["early access"]}>

One request that gives a customer a working, tested agent.

**Provisioning**
- [`POST /partner/v1/agents/provision`](/voice-engine/provisioning) finds or creates the tenant, then builds one agent per language from the business's website, the details you send and your template. English, German, French, Italian and Spanish.
- The agent is called by about ten made-up test callers, fixed and called again before it is reported ready. The test calls are not billed.
- Send your template as text, or as text with rules and capture questions pulled out.

**Jobs**
- `GET /partner/v1/jobs` and `/jobs/{jobId}` to follow long-running work, and `/jobs/{jobId}/test-report` for every test call and its verdict.
- A job that is interrupted runs again, and removes what the interrupted attempt made first.

**Receptionists**
- [`GET /partner/v1/tenants/{tenantId}/receptionist`](/voice-engine/provisioning#the-receptionist): a customer's agents as one thing, with test results, open questions and whether it is `ready` or held as `needs_review`.
- `GET /partner/v1/receptionists?status=needs_review` as a review queue, and `POST .../receptionist/approve`.
- `goLive: "approval"` holds a passing agent until you approve it.

**Events**
- `provision.started`, `provision.built`, `provision.ready`, `provision.needs_review`, `provision.failed` and `receptionist.approved`.

**Usage**
- [`GET /partner/v1/usage?groupBy=agent`](/voice-engine/usage-and-billing#usage-per-agent): usage per tenant and agent.

</Update>

<Update label="November 2026" description="Trillet-Version: 2026-10-28" tags={["early access"]}>

Finding and supporting customers, and safer retries. New fields and endpoints are additive. One existing list changes: a tenant's numbers come back 100 at a time, with `hasMore` when there is another page.

**Tenants**
- `package`, `industry` and `externalRefs` on a [tenant](/voice-engine/tenants): your own labels, and up to 10 further IDs next to `externalId`.
- [`GET /partner/v1/tenants`](/voice-engine/tenants#list-and-look-up-tenants) filters by `package`, `industry`, `externalRef`, `phoneNumber` and `q` (part of the name), and lists deleted tenants with `status=deleted`.
- [Feature switches](/voice-engine/tenants#feature-switches) per tenant: `GET` and `PATCH /partner/v1/tenants/{tenantId}/features` for the AI assistant and call reviews.

**Standard endpoints now open to partner keys**
- [Business hours](/voice-engine/call-flow-settings#business-hours) of a call flow: weekly hours, closing a date, special hours for a date, and saved schedules you can switch between.
- [Call flags](/voice-engine/support-tools#call-flags): flag a call, set its status and note, list and remove flags.
- The [issues log](/voice-engine/support-tools#issues-log).
- [Call reviews](/voice-engine/support-tools#call-reviews): reports, and the review settings of a call flow.

**Events**
- `call.flagged`, `call.flag_updated` and `call.flag_removed`.

**Conventions**
- `Idempotency-Key` is accepted on [every write](/voice-engine/conventions#idempotency), on `/partner/v1` and on the standard endpoints a partner key can call. It stays required where it was. Webhook endpoint creation and secret rotation do not take it.
- A [request limit](/voice-engine/conventions#rate-limits) of 6,000 requests a minute per key, with `RateLimit-*` response headers and `Retry-After` on `429`.
- `GET /partner/v1/tenants/{tenantId}/numbers` is paginated: 100 numbers a page at most. If a tenant has more, follow `hasMore` with `starting_after`.

**Documentation**
- New [Support tools](/voice-engine/support-tools) page.

</Update>

<Update label="October 28, 2026" description="Trillet-Version: 2026-10-28" tags={["early access"]}>

First release to design partners in early access.

**Partner keys**
- [Partner keys](/voice-engine/authentication) (`tpk_live_...`) that belong to your account, not a person, created and revoked in the **OEM API** section of account settings.
- Scopes per key, and optional restriction of a key to some tenants.
- `GET /partner/v1/key` to check a key's scopes and your tenant limit.
- A partner key can call a defined set of standard endpoints inside a tenant, with the tenant ID in `x-workspace-id`: agents, node flows, call flows, knowledge bases, numbers, calls, batch calls, call history, recordings, workflows and secrets.

**Tenants**
- [Create, list, update, suspend, resume, delete and restore](/voice-engine/tenants) a tenant per customer, keyed by your own `externalId`.
- Suspension stops a tenant's outbound calls, SMS and workflow runs, and answers and ends inbound calls, while keeping its configuration and history.
- Deleted tenants can be restored for 30 days.

**Phone numbers**
- [Buy numbers per tenant](/voice-engine/phone-numbers), billed to your partner account at the standard Trillet number price, with monthly renewal.
- Bring your own numbers over a SIP trunk or a Twilio account.
- Release numbers individually, or all at once when a tenant is deleted.

**Agents and call flows**
- Node flows created with a partner key start on the `platform-llm` model and recommended speech settings (responsiveness 10, interruption sensitivity 5.5).
- [Call flow settings](/voice-engine/call-flow-settings): read and change the greeting, speech timing, recording and call handling timers on a tenant's call flow.

**Events**
- [Signed webhooks](/voice-engine/events) in the Standard Webhooks format, for all tenants, with retries for about 22 hours, an event log kept for 30 days, and redelivery.
- Call events: `call.started`, `call.ended`, `call.analyzed`, `call.no_answer`, `call.failed` and `call.blocked`, with conversation data on by default and optional PII redaction.
- In-call events: `call.transferred`, `call.transfer_failed` and `tool.failed`.
- Booking, recording, workflow run, number, tenant and usage events.
- Recording download for a tenant's call.

**Usage and spend caps**
- [Usage](/voice-engine/usage-and-billing) totals by day or month, per tenant or per charge type, and one row per call charge.
- Monthly spend caps per tenant, with alerts at 80% and 100%. Inbound calls continue at the cap.

**Conventions**
- `Idempotency-Key` on tenant creation and number purchase and registration, so retries never create anything twice.
- One error format with stable `code` values, cursor pagination and the `Trillet-Version` response header.

**Documentation**
- A [quickstart](/voice-engine/quickstart) from partner key to first phone call, with curl, Node.js and Python.
- An [OpenAPI 3.1 specification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TrilletAI/trillet-ai-docs/main/public/openapi/trillet-oem.json) and a generated [API reference](/voice-engine/api-reference/create-a-tenant).

</Update>
