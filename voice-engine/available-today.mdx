Trillet OEM is in early access with design partners. You do not need it to start: partners already run Trillet for their customers with white-label and the standard API. This page explains that setup, what Trillet OEM adds, and what is built today.

## The setup that works today

<Steps>
  <Step title="An Agency or Enterprise account with white-label">
    Your account holds your branding: portal domain, logo, colours and email sender. Client workspaces can inherit it, so your customers see your brand, not Trillet's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="One client workspace per customer">
    Each of your customers gets its own workspace with its own agents, call flows, numbers, call history and knowledge bases. Create them in the dashboard. Each workspace can draw on your account's wallet or hold its own balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The standard API for everything inside a workspace">
    Create an API key and send the client workspace ID in `x-workspace-id` to manage that customer's agents, call flows, numbers, calls, batch calls and call history. See [Authentication](/v1/api-reference/authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Webhooks per call flow">
    Configure the post-call webhook and the [inbound call webhook](/documentation/inbound-call-webhook) on each call flow to send call results to your system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```bash
# List one customer's agents, using your API key and that customer's workspace ID
curl https://api.trillet.ai/v1/api/agents \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-workspace-id: CLIENT_WORKSPACE_ID"
```

<Note>
  A Business Associate Agreement is available on Agency and Enterprise accounts at no extra fee. Execute it before any customer workspace handles protected health information.
</Note>

## What Trillet OEM adds

| Today | With Trillet OEM |
|---|---|
| API keys belong to a person and reach every workspace that person can access | [Partner keys](/voice-engine/authentication) belong to your account, with scopes and optional tenant restrictions |
| Client workspaces are created in the dashboard | [Tenants](/voice-engine/tenants) are created, suspended, deleted and restored by API, keyed by your own customer ID, with safe retries |
| Numbers are bought per workspace | [Phone numbers](/voice-engine/phone-numbers) are bought per tenant and billed to your account, or brought over SIP or Twilio |
| Webhooks are set on each call flow | One set of [signed events](/voice-engine/events) for all tenants, with retries, an event log and redelivery |
| Usage is viewed per workspace in the dashboard | A [usage API](/voice-engine/usage-and-billing) per tenant and per call, with monthly spend caps |
| Each customer's agent is configured separately | [Templates](/voice-engine/templates) installed in every tenant and upgraded in staged rollouts (planned) |
| Your users sign in to the Trillet portal (under your brand) | [Embedded screens](/voice-engine/embedding) inside your own app, with no separate login (planned) |
