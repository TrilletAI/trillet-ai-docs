Trillet OEM, also called the Partner API, is a white-label voice AI API for software companies that want to offer AI voice agents to their own customers, under their own brand, without building the voice stack themselves. Your product stays the experience your users know. Trillet runs the telephony, the real-time voice pipeline, the agents and the call data underneath it.

It suits platforms such as practice management and EHR systems, CRMs, field-service and booking software, contact-centre platforms and learning platforms.

## How it works

You hold one **partner account**. Each of your customers gets a **tenant**: an isolated workspace with its own agents, numbers, call history and usage. Your backend manages tenants with a partner key, and your users never need a Trillet login.

## Trillet OEM or the standard API?

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Run agents for your own business | The [standard API](/v1/api-reference/introduction) with your workspace API key |
| Manage a handful of client workspaces by hand | The Trillet dashboard (Agency plan) |
| Provision and run agents for many customers from your own product | Trillet OEM |

Trillet OEM does not replace the standard API. A partner key can call a defined set of standard endpoints (agents, node flows, call flows, knowledge bases, numbers, calls, batch calls, call history, recordings, workflows and secrets) on behalf of any of its tenants. See [Authentication](/voice-engine/authentication#calling-standard-endpoints-for-a-tenant) for the list. Trillet OEM adds what a platform needs on top: tenant lifecycle, partner-billed numbers, partner-level events and usage.

## Regions

Each tenant is created in a region, `us` or `au`. Its calls, transcripts and recordings are stored in that region.

A separate sandbox environment is planned. Today, use a dedicated tenant for development and testing.
