Trillet OEM is a white-label voice AI API for platforms that serve many businesses: EHR and practice-management vendors, telecommunications providers, and software companies with hundreds, thousands or tens of thousands of clients. With one integration you can offer every one of your clients a voice agent and a phone line out of the box, under your own brand, billed to your account. Your product stays the experience your clients know. Trillet runs the telephony, the real-time voice pipeline, the agents and the call data underneath it.

## What you get

- **A workspace per client.** Each client is a tenant with its own agents, numbers, call history and usage, created and managed by API with your own customer ID.
- **Voice agents and phone numbers.** Inbound and outbound calling, numbers bought per tenant or brought over SIP, and agents built from node flows.
- **Signed events and usage.** One webhook stream for all of your tenants, usage per tenant and per call, and monthly spend caps.
- **We build it with you.** Partners work with the Trillet solutions team and have direct access to our engineers to design, launch and grow their voice offering.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Partner key">
    Your backend authenticates with a partner key that belongs to your account. See [Authentication](/voice-engine/authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="A tenant per client">
    Create a [tenant](/voice-engine/tenants) for each client, keyed by your own customer ID. Your clients never need a Trillet login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agents and numbers">
    Build the client's agent and give it a [phone number](/voice-engine/phone-numbers), using the same key with the tenant ID in `x-workspace-id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signed events">
    Receive [events](/voice-engine/events) for calls, bookings, numbers and tenants from every tenant at one endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Usage">
    Read [usage](/voice-engine/usage-and-billing) per tenant and per call, and cap each tenant's monthly spend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing

Trillet OEM partners pay the same standard rates as every Trillet customer, billed to the partner account: calls start at $0.12 per minute and phone numbers start at $4.99 per month. What you charge your own clients is up to you.

## Trillet OEM or the standard API?

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Run agents for your own business | The [standard API](/v1/api-reference/introduction) with your workspace API key |
| Manage a handful of client workspaces by hand | The Trillet dashboard (Agency plan) |
| Provision and run agents for many customers from your own product | Trillet OEM |

Trillet OEM does not replace the standard API. A partner key can call a defined set of standard endpoints (agents, node flows, call flows, knowledge bases, numbers, calls, batch calls, call history, recordings, workflows and secrets) on behalf of any of its tenants. See [Authentication](/voice-engine/authentication#calling-standard-endpoints-for-a-tenant) for the list. Trillet OEM adds what a platform needs on top: tenant lifecycle, partner-billed numbers, [call flow settings](/voice-engine/call-flow-settings), partner-level events and usage.

## Regions

Each tenant is created in a region, `us` or `au`. Its calls, transcripts and recordings are stored in that region.

A separate sandbox environment is planned. Today, use a dedicated tenant for development and testing.
